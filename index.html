It Began in Jakarta in 1996

Singaporean educator Jaspal Sidhu opened the first Singapore Intercultural School in Jakarta in 1996. He modelled it on Singapore's education system and started from one conviction: a world-class education should be within reach of the families who want it.

Thirty years on, SIS teaches in Indonesia, India, Myanmar and South Korea, and every campus still shares that promise.
