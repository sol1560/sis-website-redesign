It Began in Jakarta in 1996

Singaporean educator Jaspal Sidhu opened the first Singapore Intercultural School in Jakarta in 1996. He modelled it on Singapore's education system and started from one conviction: a world-class education should be within reach of the families who want it.

Thirty years on, SIS teaches in Indonesia, India, Myanmar and South Korea, and every campus still shares that promise.

“Quality education that is trusted, consistent, and accessible across communities and generations.”The SIS commitment

Five Core Values Guide Every Day

Fairness, Respect, Integrity, Compassion and Courage. They hang on the walls of every SIS campus, and they shape how students learn, play and treat each other.

    PACE is Part of You

    PACE names the four 21st-century skills that SIS builds into the curriculum at every campus.

    PerseveranceHelpsYouKeep Going

    What it means

    Students set goals, work steadily towards them and use feedback to improve.

    Analytical ThinkingHelpsYouAsk Why

    What it means

    Students weigh different perspectives, then test their ideas against evidence.

    Learning is

    PartofYou

    • Set a goal, then keep at it
    • Ask how you know
    • Speak up, and listen too
    • Turn ideas into real things

    Communication& CollaborationHelpsYouWork Together

    What it means

    Students express ideas clearly, in more than one language, and contribute to a team.

    Entrepreneurship& InnovationHelpsYouBuild Ideas

    What it means

    Students use design thinking to spot real problems and prototype solutions.
