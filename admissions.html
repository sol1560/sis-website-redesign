    Questions Families Ask

    Can't find your answer? Contact us and the campus team will reply.

    What happens after I book a tour?

    The admissions team at the campus you chose contacts you to confirm a time. Tours run while classes are on, so you can see a normal school day.

    How do we apply?

    Applications are made through the SIS online admissions platform. The campus team will send you the link and explain which documents they need for your child's stage.

    Which curriculum will my child study?

    SIS campuses teach Singapore and Cambridge curricula, with IB programmes at selected campuses. See Academics, or ask the campus you are interested in.

    My child is still learning English. Can they join?

    Yes. SIS offers English Bridging support. The campus team will explain how it works for your child's age and level.

    Is financial support available?

    Founder Scholarships are offered at selected campuses. Read the criteria on the Scholarships page.

    Can I ask about fees before visiting?

    Yes. Switch the form above to "General inquiry", or contact the campus directly.
